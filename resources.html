Resources & links

A working collection of the external datasets, analysis tools, and collaborator labs we draw on most often. Useful for lab members getting oriented, and for visitors who want to explore the resources behind our work.

Datasets

Datasets & cohorts

Population cohorts, brain banks, and data portals that anchor our genomics, neuroimaging, and lifespan modeling. Most require an application or data-use agreement for access.

UK Biobank ↗
500k-participant cohort: genetics, imaging, wearables, health records.
ADNI ↗
Alzheimer's Disease Neuroimaging Initiative, MRI/PET, fluid & genetic biomarkers.
ROS/MAP (RADC Hub) ↗
Rush Alzheimer's Disease Center resource-sharing hub for ROS & MAP cohorts.
AD Knowledge Portal ↗
AMP-AD multi-omic Alzheimer's data on Synapse.
ABCD Study ↗
Adolescent Brain Cognitive Development, longitudinal youth neuroimaging.
ALSPAC ↗
Avon Longitudinal Study (Children of the 90s) birth cohort.
GTEx Portal ↗
Genotype-Tissue Expression, reference eQTL & gene-expression data.
Allen Brain Map ↗
Allen Institute atlases of brain cell types and gene expression.
Psychiatric Genomics Consortium ↗
GWAS summary statistics across psychiatric disorders.
dbGaP ↗
NIH database of genotypes and phenotypes for controlled-access study data.

Methods

Methods, tools & software

Core software for the analyses we run most: polygenic scoring, single-cell and multi-omic integration, neuroimaging pipelines, and machine learning.

Genetics & polygenic scores

PLINK ↗
Whole-genome association and population-genetics toolset.
PRSice-2 ↗
Polygenic risk score calculation and visualization.
PRS-CS ↗
Bayesian polygenic prediction with continuous shrinkage priors.
LDSC ↗
LD score regression for heritability & genetic correlation.
FUMA ↗
Functional mapping and annotation of GWAS results.
MAGMA ↗
Gene- and gene-set-level analysis of GWAS data.

Single-cell & multi-omics

metasnf ↗
Lab-affiliated R package: meta-clustering with similarity network fusion.
Seurat ↗
R toolkit for single-cell RNA-seq analysis.
Scanpy ↗
Scalable Python toolkit for single-cell analysis.
Bioconductor ↗
R packages for the analysis of high-throughput genomic data.

Neuroimaging

FreeSurfer ↗
Cortical reconstruction and structural MRI morphometry.
FSL ↗
FMRIB software library for structural, functional & diffusion MRI.
fMRIPrep ↗
Robust, reproducible functional MRI preprocessing pipeline.
Nilearn ↗
Machine learning for neuroimaging in Python.
ENIGMA Toolbox ↗
Tools and datasets for the ENIGMA consortium analyses.

Machine learning & statistics

scikit-learn ↗
General-purpose machine learning in Python.
The R Project ↗
Environment for statistical computing and graphics.
